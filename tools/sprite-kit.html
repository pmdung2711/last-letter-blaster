Pixel sprites

Each sprite is a string grid in the GDD palette format (. O M S W F) and gets its colours by palette swap, matching decision 0001 (no image files). You can paste a grid straight into game.js. If you ever need image files, you can also export PNGs or a sprite sheet with atlas JSON.
